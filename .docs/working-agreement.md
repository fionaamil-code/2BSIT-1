# Team Working Agreement

## Communication
- Main channel: Telegram and Gmail
- Reply to messages within 24 hours. If you will be away, post it in the channel.
- Task discussion happens in the GitHub issue, so decisions are recorded.

## Meetings and updates
- Daily update (asynchronously in the channel): what I did, what I will do, any blockers.
- Mid-sprint check on Sprint Day 5.
- Sprint review and retrospective on Sprint Day 10.

## Working on tasks
- One task, one issue, one branch, one PR.
- Every PR links its issue with `Closes #n` and uses the PR template.
- Nobody pushes directly to `main`. Every PR needs approval from a teammate who is not the author.
- Review requests are answered within 6-12 hours.
- Tell the Scrum Master about a blocker as soon as you find it, not on the due date.

## Respect and fairness
- Share the workload fairly. Ask for help early.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Missed commitments are raised with the team first, then the Scrum Master, then the instructor.

## Changing this agreement
Any member can propose a change in a PR. The change is merged once the team agrees.
